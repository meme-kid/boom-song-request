Each scan reads the Daily trend (EMA20/50) and H4 structure (swing highs/lows, RSI, ATR) from live PAXG/USD gold data, then builds a swing call at the real support/resistance zone — the same market gives the same call, no randomness. Live price auto-manages TP1 → breakeven → scale-in; the two Test buttons just preview those states.

Educational tool — not financial advice. Signals are rules-based swing reads on live PAXG/USD gold data (a spot-gold proxy), not guaranteed trades. Engine is modular (SignalEngine.scan, PositionManager, PriceFeed) — swap the data source or rules for your own.
